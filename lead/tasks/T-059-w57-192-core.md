## Context, goal, benefit, cost and stop rule

The Owner authorizes a NEW independent W57 study with 192 acquisition starts, from T0 through T0+47h45, with response completion no later than T0+48h. Old studies and their conclusions remain immutable and inconclusive. Every other existing scientific/pilot gate stays fixed. T054 deliberately retained the old 193-start rule; merely skipping its last workflow step would leave incorrect core terminal state, denominators and grace behavior.

Add one explicit immutable protocol capsule and thread it through existing scientific functions. Preserve default LEGACY193 behavior, public constants and v1/v2 historical interpretation. Do not duplicate the entire driver or monkeypatch its globals. This protects the new measurement's inference boundary. Engineering cost is one bounded core task; stop at reviewed deterministic core compatibility, leaving session operation and any real request to T060.
