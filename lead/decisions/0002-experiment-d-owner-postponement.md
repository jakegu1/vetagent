# ADR-0002: Owner postpones Experiment D to 2026-10-20

Date: 2026-10-16
Status: accepted by the Owner's explicit instruction

## Decision

Move the Experiment D decision gate and its dependent parked-opportunity review dates
from 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-20. Keep the existing UTC date interpretation.

The experiment remains pending. Its twenty-operator, USD99/month proposition and at least
three willing-trial-commitment requirement are unchanged. No conclusion or customer result
is inferred from the postponement.

Unrelated engineering deadlines, later product gates and historical measurements stay as
recorded. Current technical repairs may proceed under their existing invariants.
Changing the due date again remains an Owner decision.
