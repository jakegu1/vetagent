## Why engineering alone cannot max this out

Demand 15 + external callers 5 + measurable recall 10 = **30 points**,
and none of the three **can be earned by writing code**:

- Demand needs someone willing to pay
- External callers needs someone to actually wire it in
- Measurable recall needs the snapshot store to collect enough dead samples, and time is not for sale

So **the ceiling for pure engineering is 70**.
That is not pessimism baked into the design, it is the reason this score exists —
**it does not let 'I have been busy' impersonate 'we made progress'.**
